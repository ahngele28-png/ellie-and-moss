A Note from the Maker

A little of where I'm from.
A little of who I am.

I'm Angela, the artist and maker behind Ellie & Moss. I paint, shape wire, play with color, and follow an idea to see where it takes me.

Ellie is for my grandmother. Moss is for the Spanish moss hanging from the trees around Savannah. Together, they hold a little of home.

That feeling runs through the work, from a memory caught in acrylic painting to a piece of jewelry shaped by hand.

Made with my hands. Seen through my eyes.
