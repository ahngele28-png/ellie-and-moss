About Me

Made by Hand, Raised in the South

I'm a jewelry maker with a deep love for shaping wire and metal into something you'd actually want to wear every day. Growing up in the South put creativity at the center of everything I do, and it still shows up in every piece.

Nothing here comes off a production line. Every earring and pendant is bent, hammered, or wrapped by hand, which means no two pieces turn out exactly the same. I like it that way.

Jewelry should tell a story, not just complete an outfit.
